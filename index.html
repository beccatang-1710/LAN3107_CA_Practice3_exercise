  1. Greet your colleague and introduce yourself
  2. Make small talk on the following topics:
    • A useful app or tool you've been using for work lately
    • A project run by your department — Promoting a new AI-powered content creation tool through social media

      • what has been done so far

      • plans for the coming months

  3. Share your opinions on a new company policy and support your views with reasons

    • the potential benefits and drawbacks the policy may bring to the company and its employees

  4. End the conversation politely
